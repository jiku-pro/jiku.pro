JIKU

Jiku is an open-source software company specializing in the advanced statistical analysis of continuum data.

Our main products are Jiku Core and Jiku UI (both in development). Our first fully released app is Jiku Power1D.

Jiku Power1D

Statistical power analysis...
for one-dimensional data

Power1D is the first-ever app that conducts power analysis on continuous 1D datasets (time series, waveforms, biomechanical trajectories, etc.). Power1D supports (1) interactive 1D effect construction, (2) flexible noise modeling, (3) sample size analysis, and (4) automated post hoc power analysis of experimental datasets.
